About Me

I started studying Web and User Experience Design because I was tired of using apps and websites that felt confusing or frustrating — and I wanted to be part of the solution. With a background in graphic design, photography, printmaking, business, and multimedia, I bring a creative and practical approach to designing digital experiences.

Over the past few years, I’ve developed skills in front-end development, user testing, and solving design problems with user-centered solutions. I especially enjoy making things intuitive.

I loved learning React and JavaScript, conducting surveys, running user tests, and creating prototypes that helped real people accomplish real goals. While I care about aesthetics, I care even more about function — users should always know where to go and how to get there without second-guessing themselves.

Looking forward, I hope to use my skills to design tools and resources that make life easier, especially for families, small businesses, and communities of faith. I believe good design should feel like grace — helpful, clear, and quietly supportive behind the scenes.

“Design is intelligence made visible.” — Alina Wheeler
